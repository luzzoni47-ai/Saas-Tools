Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about slugs, permalinks and clean links.

What exactly is a URL slug?

A URL slug is the human-readable portion of a web address that identifies a specific page. In https://example.com/url-slug-generator, the slug is url-slug-generator.

Should I use hyphens or underscores?

Hyphens. Google treats hyphens as word separators, so best-seo-tools reads as three distinct words. Underscores join words together.

How long should a slug be?

Aim for 3–5 meaningful words and keep the total under roughly 60 characters. The quality score turns green in that sweet spot.

What are SEO stop words?

Low-value filler words such as a, an, the, and, or, in, on, of, to. Stripping them produces shorter, higher-density slugs.

Does it handle accented and non-Latin text?

Yes. Latin characters are normalized to ASCII, and Cyrillic and Greek are transliterated. The "Keep non-Latin" switch preserves CJK intact.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely client-side. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored.
