Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about HEIC files and this converter.

Is this HEIC to JPG converter really free?
Yes — completely. Unlimited conversions, no watermarks, no account, no ads, no "premium" upsell. The tool runs on your own hardware, so there is no server bill to pass on to you.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Decoding and encoding happen inside your browser using the Canvas API. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the converter keeps working — the strongest possible proof that nothing leaves your device.
How do I convert HEIC photos on Windows?
Windows cannot open HEIC natively without a paid codec. Simply open this page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, drag your HEIC files in, choose JPG and download. No software installation required.
Does converting HEIC to JPG lose quality?
At 90–100% quality the difference is visually imperceptible. If you need a mathematically lossless result, choose PNG output. The quality slider lets you balance fidelity against file size in real time.
Is there a batch size limit?
There is no artificial limit. Because processing is local, the only ceiling is your device's memory. Most laptops comfortably handle several hundred photos in one go.
What is a HEIC file anyway?
HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format on iPhones since iOS 11. It stores the same visual quality as JPG in roughly half the space, but compatibility outside Apple's ecosystem is patchy — which is why converting to JPG is so common.
Is my EXIF and location data removed?
Yes. Re-encoding through a canvas produces a clean image without embedded EXIF metadata, GPS coordinates or device identifiers — an extra layer of privacy when sharing photos publicly.
