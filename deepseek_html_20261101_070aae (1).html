FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes — completely. There are no paid tiers, no watermarks, no export limits and no account to create. The entire tool runs in your browser, so there are no server costs to pass on to you.

How do I save my invoice as a PDF?

Click Download PDF (or press ⌘/Ctrl + P). Your browser's print dialog opens with a clean stylesheet applied — choose Save as PDF as the destination. The text stays selectable and vector-sharp, and file sizes are typically under 200 KB.

Is my client data private?

Yes. There is no backend. Invoice fields are saved only in your browser's local storage so you don't lose work on refresh. Clearing your browser data removes them permanently.

Can I use this invoice for VAT, GST or sales tax?

Absolutely. Set any tax rate, add your Tax/VAT ID in the business section, and include bank or IBAN details. Discounts can be applied as a percentage or a flat amount before tax.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AUD, CAD, AED, JPY, CHF and BRL are built in. Amounts format with two decimals and thousands separators automatically.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout stacks form and preview vertically on small screens, and the sticky action bar keeps export one tap away.
